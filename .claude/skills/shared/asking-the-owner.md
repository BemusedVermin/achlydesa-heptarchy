# Asking the owner

How the decide and triage skills put choices to the owner. This folder has no SKILL.md, so it isn't a skill of its own.

- **Prepare each item first.** Read its task file, the docs and code it points at, and what depends on it, before writing the question.
- **Each question stands alone.** He may be on his phone and see only the question dialog. Say what the item is and why it matters in a sentence or two: name the forms, parts, rules or packages involved, and what the game or code does today. No "as above", and no ID without a description.
- **Two or three real options,** each with its consequence in one line, such as what the player would notice, what work it starts, or what it costs. Put your recommendation first, with "(Recommended)" at the end of its label. Recommend only what the docs and the milestone plan support.
- **"Not now" last,** for leaving the item as it is.
- **Ask with AskUserQuestion,** up to four questions per call, in batches until all are asked. Keep each header under 12 characters, such as the part or rule the question is about.
- **His own words count.** He can type an answer instead of picking one; treat it as his decision, and record it as he gave it.
