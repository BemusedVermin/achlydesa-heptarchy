# M6-17.F1 · Stop the go_to picker searching a whole cell path at every step

## Goal

A near agent walking to a landmark costs about one short search per step, not two full ones, so 100 near agents fit the phase budget.

## Context

- `internal/core/agents/movement.go`, `goToPicker.Pick`: every step runs `w.Map.Path` with `walkNodes` (200) expansions, and runs it again without the held-cell price when the first route fails or passes a held cell. When bodies crowd round a well, both searches run out their 200 nodes at every decision.
- `BenchmarkPhaseWithFarAgents` (`internal/core/agents/lod_bench_test.go`): `goToPicker.Pick` is about 40% of a phase, and the far agents about 3%.

## Deliverables

- `internal/core/agents/movement.go`: keep the route a go_to step found (on the mind, or a component) and follow it, searching again only when the next cell is blocked or the target moved; or bound the second search tighter.
- The benchmark's number in M6-17's Notes, measured again.

## Spec

- **Firm:** the route a body walks stays deterministic and the same with or without a save between steps (a kept route is saved, or rebuilt the same way).
- **Suggestion:** store the remaining cells in the mind component, dropped with the plan.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `BenchmarkPhaseWithFarAgents` is at least a third faster.
- [ ] The movement tests still pass.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.
