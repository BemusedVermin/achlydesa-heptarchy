# M5-05.F1 · Stop wall-less place layers from sprouting a tunnel per cell

## Goal

Decide which open place cells are openings, so a place's wall-less lower layers do not each carve a tunnel.

## Context

- `internal/core/worldgen/source.go`, `openingsOf`: an opening is any open place cell with a horizontal neighbor outside the places, as the spec words it.
- The test descent has layers with no walls (the soop at depths 1 to 4 and the ledge at depth 2), so every cell there is an opening: 33 openings on the sample world, most of them along those soops, each with its own tunnel.

## Spec

Options: keep the literal rule (cheap, noisy); require the place to mark its openings in content (authors know where a place meets the deep, but every place needs marks); merge openings within a few cells into one tunnel (no content change, fuzzier). Recommend marking in content.

**Decided by the owner:** Mark in content. A place's author marks its exits; a wall-less edge is never an exit, and a place with no marks stays closed. This is what M5-05 shipped (`openings` in the place file), so no code or content changes follow.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The sample places carve tunnels only where the author means a way out.

## Out of scope

- Guaranteeing reach: M5-07.

## Notes

Raised by M5-05. M5-05 already implemented the decided option at the owner's direction, so nothing is left to build; closed as done. The decision is recorded in `docs/game-design.md` → "Procgen and exports".
