# M2-07.F1.F1 · Wake at the last rest town after a party wipe

## Goal

When every member of the party is dead or downed, the party wakes at the town it last rested in, with some time consumed, instead of `PartyLost` ending the game.

## Context

- The owner's decision (recorded in `docs/game-design.md`, "Three tiers of people"): the deuteragonists wake at the town they last rested in, with some time consumed.
- M2-07's placeholder: `combat.PartyLost` and `text.game.lost` in `game.Apply` (`internal/core/combat/death.go`, `internal/core/game/game.go`).
- M2-07.F3 makes a wipe inside one `Apply` stop the timeline cleanly; this builds on it.

## Deliverables

- Record the last-rested town (a component or resource, saved).
- On a wipe, move the party to that town, restore them as downed members get up, and advance the clock by a content-set number of ticks.
- The amount of time and any other cost are content numbers.

## Spec

Open design points, to file as questions if they matter: what the enemies did meanwhile, whether dead (not downed) members come back, and what happens before any rest. Most reversible meanwhile: start at the opening town, and revive downed members only.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A test wipes the party; it wakes in the last rest town, the clock has advanced, and play continues.
- [x] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Story scenes for the waking.

## Notes

Filed by M2-15 from the owner's decision on M2-07.F1. Triaged by M2-15: todo in M3, because the story and towns it needs arrive then, and it follows M2's last task.

Done by M2-07.F1.F1. A wipe is handled at the end of `game.Apply` (`wakeIfWiped`): the clock advances by `party.wake_ticks` (600, placeholder), then `explore.Wake` mends downed members, puts them back on the timeline under their template controller and sets every living member around the rest cell. `comp.RestPlace` on the party is saved with the world; `explore.Start` sets it to the opening cell, since no towns or rest action exist. Most reversible choices: only downed members revive (a party of the dead stays lost with `text.game.lost`), enemies stay put and do not act during the lost time, no other cost. Added `api.PartyWoke`, `text.explore.party_woke`, `PartyConfig.WakeTicks`; schemas regenerated; `docs/game-design.md` updated.
