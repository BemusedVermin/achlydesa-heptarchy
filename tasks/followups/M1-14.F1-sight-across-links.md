# M1-14.F1 · Sight and pathing across map links

## Goal

`Visible` and `Path` follow links to other maps (folds, portals) once a topology produces them, so a body sees and walks through a fold the way it steps through one.

## Context

- M1-14 keeps both on one map: `Visible` walks a straight hex line, and `Path` skips any step whose `Link.Map` is another map.
- `Map.Step` already returns such steps as given; `Map.solid` treats them as solid for standing.

## Deliverables

- `internal/core/hexmap/sight.go`, `path.go`: cross-map handling, once folds exist.

## Spec

Open design question: a straight line has no meaning across an impossible fold. Options: (a) never see through links (today's behavior, cheapest); (b) trace the line step by step through `Topology.Neighbor`, switching maps at links; (c) pathing only. Recommended: (c) first, then (b) when the first fold ships.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A route crosses a link between two maps in a test.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

- Drawing what is seen (M1-10).
