# M1-10.F1.F1 · Plan view: draw by reachability, not a depth window

## Goal

Decide whether the plan view should show the areas the party can actually reach, with elevation shown by tone (`Below`), and if so replace the depth window M1-10.F1 added with a reachability rule.

## Context

- M1-10.F1 added `party.plan_floor_depth` (4 for now). In `nearest` in `internal/core/explore/project.go`, a hex's wall candidates are ignored when a standable cell of that hex lies within that many depths of the focus, above or below.
- That is a depth window around the focus. It is not reachability: it never checks whether the party can step to the cell, and `party.max_step_up` and `party.max_step_down` play no part in it.
- Intent as the owner stated it: render the accessible areas, with elevation indicated by tone. M1-10.F1's "slope hexes must render as floors" criterion was read too literally; the window is a stand-in for that intent.
- Cases where the two differ: a standable ledge within 4 depths but unreachable (a shelf above a cliff) is drawn as floor and hides the cliff's wall; a reachable floor more than 4 depths away still loses to nearer walls.

## Deliverables

- A decision on what "accessible" means here (reachable from the focus by `hexmap` pathing within the party's step limits, with or without a range cap, and whether remembered cells count).
- The change to `explore/project.go` and its tests, and removal or reuse of `party.plan_floor_depth`.

## Spec

Options (a suggestion; the owner decides):

1. **Keep the window.** Cheap, tunable, already in. Cost: the cases above.
2. **Reachable set from the focus.** Run pathing over the visible columns and let a hex draw its reachable standable cell; draw walls only where nothing is reachable. Cost: pathing work on every view, and a rule for seen-but-not-visible cells.
3. **Window plus step limits.** Keep the window but count a standable cell only if it is within `max_step_up` and `max_step_down` of a neighbour already counted. Cheaper than full pathing; still approximate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The owner has chosen an option.
- [ ] Walking `map.test.descent` up and down its slope draws the accessible cells with `Below` changing smoothly, in both directions.
- [ ] An unreachable ledge within the old window does not hide the wall below or beside it.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Sight and pathing across map links (M1-14.F1).
- Frontend tone choices for `Below`.
