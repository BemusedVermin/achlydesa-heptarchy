# M1-17.F1 · timeline: reschedule bodies when a hook's answer changes

## Goal

A body's next decision follows its usable channels even when the change didn't come through `Occupy`, `Extend` or `Wait`: a part is lost, a grant is added, a status stops a channel being usable.

## Context

- M1-17 queues a body at its decision tick when it occupies a channel, extends one or waits. At pop time it recomputes the tick and requeues the body if the tick moved later.
- It cannot notice a decision that should now come earlier, because nothing requeues the body.
- Callers will be M2-02 (parts lost or attached) and M1-20 (usable changes).

## Deliverables

- `internal/core/timeline`: an exported `Reschedule(w *sim.World, body ecs.Entity)` that recomputes the body's decision tick and requeues it.
- A test where `SetUsable` starts rejecting the only busy channel and `Reschedule` brings the decision forward.

## Spec

`Reschedule` is the only addition (suggested name). It does nothing for a body with no `comp.Actor`.
