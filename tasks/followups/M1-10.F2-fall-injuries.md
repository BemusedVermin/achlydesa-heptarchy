# M1-10.F2 · Injuries from falls

## Goal

Dropping off a ledge hurts the legs, so a fall has a price beyond the lost position.

## Context

- M1-10 publishes `api.Fell{Actor, Cells}` when a step is a drop, and does nothing else with it. The task said to link the injury to leg damage in M2.
- M2-02 makes legs parts with their own damage.

## Deliverables

- A trigger (M2-05) or effect on `explore.fell` that damages the faller's legs by an amount from content, scaled by `Cells`.
- Content for the amounts, and a test that a three-cell drop injures and a one-cell step down does not.

## Spec

Content-defined: a trigger on `explore.fell`, so no code in `explore`. The numbers are placeholders for M7.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A drop of three cells damages the faller's legs; a step down does not.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Fall damage to bodies that are not legged, and falling into water.
