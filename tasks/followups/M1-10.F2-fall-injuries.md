# M1-10.F2 · Injuries from falls

## Goal

Dropping off a ledge hurts the legs, so a fall has a price beyond the lost position.

## Context

- M1-10 publishes `api.Fell{Actor, Cells}` when a step is a drop, and does nothing else with it. The task said to link the injury to leg damage in M2.
- M2-02 makes legs parts with their own damage.

## Deliverables

- A trigger (M2-05) or effect on `explore.fell` that damages the faller's legs by an amount from content, scaled by `Cells`.
- Content for the amounts, and a test that a three-cell drop injures and a one-cell step down does not.

## Spec

Content-defined: a trigger on `explore.fell`, so no code in `explore`. The numbers are placeholders for M7.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A drop of three cells damages the faller's legs; a step down does not.
- [x] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Fall damage to bodies that are not legged, and falling into water.

## Notes

Triaged by the owner: stays an M2 task, to start after M2-05 and M2-02.

**Assumptions and deviations**

- `api.Fell` implements `Subjected` (the faller is the target) and `Chosen` (`cells`); `EffectEnv` gained `cells`, so formulas can scale by the drop.
- The trigger sits on the two person templates through one shared CUE definition (`_fallInjury`): template triggers are per template, and a body-wide trigger needs a carrier. Placeholders: a drop of two cells or more costs the legs `cells - 1` integrity. The `Fell` event has no source, so the damage selector is `target:legs`.
- A one-cell fall is not published by explore today (only drops are), so the test publishes `api.Fell{Cells: 1}` directly to show the condition leaves it alone.
- Bodies without legs are untouched (empty selector), as the out-of-scope list asks.

**Files outside the Deliverables**

`internal/content/env.go` and its generated schema, `internal/core/api/explore.go`, `internal/core/effects/effects.go`, `content/data/templates.cue`, `internal/core/explore/explore_test.go`.
