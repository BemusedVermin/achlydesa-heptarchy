# M6-22.F1 · Let the geography place landmark pieces in the deep

## Goal

A geography stage places a piece (a lake, a coal seam) at generated positions in the deep, joined by `join` and checked by `verify`, so a resource sits where the ground puts it rather than at a fixed coordinate.

## Context

- M6-22 asked for the Reaper's water as a `piece.lake` placed by the geography as a landmark in the deep. No geography stage places pieces; only settlements and places are fixed ground.
- M6-22 stands in a one-piece settlement, `settlement.reaper_lake`, at a fixed position near the Reaper's crawlspace.
- `docs/game-design.md` "Procgen and exports": materials appear where they belong (coal near dead crawlspaces).

## Deliverables

- A `landmarks` stage in `internal/core/worldgen` that stamps pieces at seeded positions within a depth band and a distance from named settlements, never changed by an export.
- Replace `settlement.reaper_lake` with it.

## Spec

Open design point: whether such landmarks may also appear far from any town. Recommend no, so the first version only places them near a settlement.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The Reaper's water lies outside its walls with no settlement record for it.
- [x] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Residents hauling from it: M6-23.

## Notes

- Not a chunk stage. A `Stage` only changes one chunk's terrain and its params are numbers, but a landmark needs a piece, a settlement, spawns and openings known before `join` runs. So a geography gains `landmarks` (`content.GeographyLandmark`: `piece`, `near`, `min_distance`/`max_distance`, `min_depth`/`max_depth`), `worldgen.PlaceLandmark` draws the position from the world seed alone (no export count), and `explore.WorldSource` stamps each one after the settlements as fixed ground, with the piece's doors as its footprint's openings. `join` and `verify` then treat it like any place.
- A draw fits when every cell is inside the world, outside the settlement's radius, and neither on nor beside a covered cell (so it can't block an exit). After 256 failed draws, building the world fails. The 256 is an engine limit, not a tuning number.
- The content loader checks the piece and settlement exist, that `min_distance` is above the settlement's radius plus one, and both bands. `contenttest.Fixture` drops landmarks along with settlements.
- The open design point is taken as recommended: a landmark always lies near a settlement.
- Content: `settlement.reaper_lake` and its text are gone. `geography.deep` places `piece.lake` 40 to 60 hexes from the Reaper's centre at depth 5 to 7 (first values).
- Tests: `TestTheReapersCrawlspaceHoldsNoWaterSource` now checks seeds 1 to 4 for a water source outside the walls and near the town, and that no settlement but the lake crawlspace lays a lake. `TestEveryRealTownConnectsToTheDeep` also checks the lake's openings. `TestGeographyLandmarksAreChecked` covers the loader checks.
- `go tool task check` was run as `check-ci BASE=origin/master`, which tested the 21 affected packages and ran every other check.
- Docs: `docs/content-guide.md` gains "A landmark in the deep". `docs/glossary.md` gains **landmark piece**, so the geography's `landmarks` aren't mistaken for affordance landmarks. If you'd rather rename the field (to `set_pieces`, say), that's a small change.
- A landmark piece's footprint, like its marks, is named by its piece ID. Two landmarks of the same piece would share a name in `join` and `verify` errors.
- Outside the list: `internal/content/drives_go_gen.cue` picked up a `Decay` field that was missing on master when `go tool task schema` ran.
