# M6-22.F1 · Let the geography place landmark pieces in the deep

## Goal

A geography stage places a piece (a lake, a coal seam) at generated positions in the deep, joined by `join` and checked by `verify`, so a resource sits where the ground puts it rather than at a fixed coordinate.

## Context

- M6-22 asked for the Reaper's water as a `piece.lake` placed by the geography as a landmark in the deep. No geography stage places pieces; only settlements and places are fixed ground.
- M6-22 stands in a one-piece settlement, `settlement.reaper_lake`, at a fixed position near the Reaper's crawlspace.
- `docs/game-design.md` "Procgen and exports": materials appear where they belong (coal near dead crawlspaces).

## Deliverables

- A `landmarks` stage in `internal/core/worldgen` that stamps pieces at seeded positions within a depth band and a distance from named settlements, never changed by an export.
- Replace `settlement.reaper_lake` with it.

## Spec

Open design point: whether such landmarks may also appear far from any town. Recommend no, so the first version only places them near a settlement.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Reaper's water lies outside its walls with no settlement record for it.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Residents hauling from it: M6-23.
