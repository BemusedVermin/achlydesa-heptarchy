# M4-03.F1 · Recruit personality: robbery, desertion and loyalty

## Goal

What a recruit does as its pay goes unmet depends on its personality: one asks for its pay and then quits, another robs the party on the way out, and loyalty keeps some around longer.

## Context

- `docs/game-design.md` → "Party" → "Recruits" → "Paying them" (the owner's decision while planning M4, 2026-11-25: "Pay need, with higher levels of need leading to grumbling and then desertion or robbery depending on personality. Loyalty might also keep people around. But for now keep it simple.").
- M3's recruits were reverted (M4-01). M6 adds recruits back as agents, and this fits there.
- `docs/game-design.md` → "Grudges and memory" (M8).

## Deliverables

- To be worked out when triaged: robbing the party as a planner action that personality makes attractive, and loyalty as a desire that grows with time served.

## Spec

To be written when triaged. Outcomes must stay behaviors (AGENTS.md rule 10).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two recruits with different personalities, both unpaid, leave in different ways, through their desires and the planner only.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Grudges a deserter holds: M8's grudges and memory.
