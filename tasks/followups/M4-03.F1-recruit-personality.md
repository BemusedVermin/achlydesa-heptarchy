# M4-03.F1 · Recruit personality: robbery, desertion and loyalty

## Goal

What a recruit does as its pay goes unmet depends on its personality: one grumbles and walks off, another robs the party on the way out, and loyalty keeps some around longer.

## Context

- `docs/game-design.md` → "Party" → "Recruits" → "Paying them" (the owner's decision while planning M4, 2026-11-25: "Pay need, with higher levels of need leading to grumbling and then desertion or robbery depending on personality. Loyalty might also keep people around. But for now keep it simple.").
- M4-03 builds the simple version: `need.pay`, and the `grumble` and `leave_party` behaviors.
- `docs/game-design.md` → "Grudges and memory" (M7).

## Deliverables

- To be worked out when triaged: personality as template data or a per-body trait, a `rob` behavior taking from the party's inventory before `leave_party`, and loyalty as a need or a consideration that rises with time served.

## Spec

To be written when triaged. Outcomes must stay behaviors (AGENTS.md rule 10).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two recruits with different personalities, both unpaid, leave in different ways, through behaviors only.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Grudges a deserter holds: M7's grudges and memory.

## Notes

Filed while planning M4, from the owner's answer on how recruits deal with the party.
