# M2-11.F2 · Where do the sample's three creatures spawn?

## Goal

Decide whether the remnant and the flesh beast spawn on `map.test.hall` with the crawler, as M2-11's Deliverables say, or stay on the separate `map.test.den`, where M2-11 put them.

## Context

- M2-11 Deliverables: three enemy templates "and their spawns on `map.test.hall`". Its lethality target speaks of "the hall's creature" in the singular.
- With all three hunters in the hall, a random-walking test party dies in the harness, save and frontend tests, and the lethality fight has three enemies instead of one. M2-11 chose the reversible option: the hall keeps the crawler, and `content/data/maps/test_den.cue` holds the remnant and the beast.

## Deliverables

- If the owner wants all three in the hall: add the marks and spawns to `test_hall.cue`, keep the other tests standing (rest controllers, as the harness and save tests do for the crawler), and retune the lethality numbers.

## Spec

Options: (a) keep the den, which costs nothing and leaves every hall test as it is; (b) move all three to the hall, which costs edits to about six test fixtures and a retune of lethality. Recommendation: (a).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The owner has chosen, and the content and Notes of M2-11 match.
