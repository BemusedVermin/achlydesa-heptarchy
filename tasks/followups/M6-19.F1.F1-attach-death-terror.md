# M6-19.F1.F1 · Attach the death-terror trigger to the templates whose deaths others see

## Goal

A body's death raises terror in the minds that see it in real play, not only in a test.

## Context

- `content/data/templates.cue` defines `_terrorOnDeath` (on `combat.body_died`, role `target`, `satisfy` on `witnesses`), attached to nothing.
- The trigger goes on the template of the body that dies, not the witness. So it belongs on every template whose death a mind should fear (persons and creatures alike), which is wider than M6-19.F2's list of mind templates.
- M6-19.F2 attaches `_terrorOnHit` and says the neighbour's death belongs to M6-19.F1; it does not cover this trigger. It could be folded into F2 when triaging.
- Adding a trigger to an existing template changes its saved trigger IDs and the goldens.

## Deliverables

- `_terrorOnDeath` in the `triggers` of each template whose death should frighten those who see it, in `content/data/templates.cue`.

## Spec

Which templates, and the amount (placeholder `-3`, so +3 terror), are tuning.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In a started game, a mind beside a body killed in combat has a higher drive.safety level after it.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Whether the dead's kin or faction should fear more than strangers: a design question for later.
