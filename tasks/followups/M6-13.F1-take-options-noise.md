# M6-13.F1 · Hide take options for items the thing does not hold

## Goal

The player's menu lists a take only for items the source or container holds, and a put only into a thing that has room, instead of a disabled option for every ware.

## Context

- `action.take` offers every ware (the `ware` choice) for every source or container in reach; one the thing lacks comes out disabled with `text.actions.none_there`. Next to a well that is a disabled "Take ration" beside the real "Take water".
- Choices are offered per actor (`internal/core/actions/choices.go`), so they cannot see the target.

## Deliverables

- `internal/core/actions/`: drop an intent from the options when an effect's check says the target can never give the choice (an `Impossible` kind of refusal), or offer choices per target.
- A test that a well offers no ration.

## Spec

Whichever route is taken, an agent's picker and the player's menu must keep agreeing on what is startable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Next to a well the menu has take water and no take ration.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Container capacity: containers have no cap today, so only a source can be full.
