# M6-06.F1 · Split chunk graph nodes by the walkable areas inside each chunk

## Goal

A route on the chunk graph only passes through a chunk where the opening it enters by and the one it leaves by are joined inside that chunk, so far agents never take a way that does not exist.

## Context

- `internal/core/worldgen/graph.go`: `Graph.Neighbors` and `Graph.Route` treat every chunk as joining all its openings. Verify joins the whole ground, so a target is reachable when the route says so, but the walk may need a detour through other chunks, and the cost can be well under the real walk.
- `internal/core/worldgen/verify.go`: `reader.scan` already flood-fills a chunk into local areas; the same split could tag each opening with the area of its From cell and its To cell.
- `Graph.Route` to an unreachable chunk summarizes every chunk the search reaches, which on a 400 × 400 world is thousands of chunks. Areas would not change that; a reach limit like `hexmap.Map.Path`'s `maxNodes` might.

## Deliverables

- Openings carry the local area of each end; graph nodes become (chunk, area); `Route` still returns chunk keys and a cost.
- An optional node limit on `Route`.
- Tests: a chunk whose two openings lie in separate areas is not crossed between them.

## Spec

- **Firm:** summaries stay pure functions of the seed and export count, computed on other goroutines and applied in chunk-key order.
- **Suggestion:** keep a per-chunk area table only for the border cells, not all 4096 slots.
