# M5-10.F2 · Give a sealed pocket a way out

## Goal

An export with the party in a chamber that no walk leaves still leaves a route to a town.

## Context

- `pocketExits` in `internal/core/explore/export.go` walks from the party inside the pocket and takes the standable cells that border new ground. A chamber the party was dropped into, with no walkable edge, has none, so the pocket has no openings and the party is shut in.
- `docs/game-design.md` ("An export with the party below") promises a route to at least one town.

## Deliverables

- A decision, then the change.

## Spec

Options:

1. Open a tunnel from the pocket cell nearest its edge: keeps the promise, but edits the pocket the design says is kept as it was.
2. Leave it: the party digs or waits for the next export; costs the promise in rare cases.
3. Widen the pocket until it has an edge cell: keeps the pocket unchanged but keeps more of the old map.

Recommendation: 3, capped by the chunk budget.

**Decided by the owner:** Widen the pocket (option 3). When the party's chamber has no walkable edge, the pocket grows over the surrounding old ground until it has an edge cell, capped by the chunk budget, so the pocket's own cells stay as they were and a route to a town remains. `docs/game-design.md` now says so.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A test with a sealed chamber has a route out after an export.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Verify over the large world: M5-07.F1.

## Notes

Filed by M5-10.

Blocked by M5-10.F2.F1: widening the pocket cannot give a closed chamber a way out. `pocketExits` takes any reached standable cell with a neighbor outside the pocket, so the exit list is empty only when the walkable region the party stands in is closed. Taking in more old ground adds cells the party cannot reach, so the list stays empty at any radius. Only a change to the pocket's own cells (option 1) can open a route.
