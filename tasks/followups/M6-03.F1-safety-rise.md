# M6-03.F1 · Decide what raises drive.safety

## Goal

Settle how the safety need grows, so its `rise` and thresholds in `content/data/drives.cue` mean something.

## Context

- `docs/game-design.md` → "How minds work" → "Drives" lists safety among the needs, and `docs/glossary.md` defines a need as "a drive that rises with time and can kill".
- Feeling unsafe does not obviously grow with time alone, and M6-19 plans `drive.terror`, raised by triggers when a body is hit or a neighbour dies.
- M6-03 shipped `drive.safety` as a need with `rise: "0"` and no thresholds, so its level only moves through the `satisfy` effect.

## Deliverables

- An answer recorded in `docs/game-design.md` ("Drives") and, if it changes, the glossary's definition of a need.
- `content/data/drives.cue`: `drive.safety` changed to match.

## Spec

Options:

1. **Safety rises with time away from shelter** (a rise formula that reads something like "in the open"). Costs a new `DriveEnv` variable the drive tick fills in, and a notion of shelter. Keeps "every need rises with time" true.
2. **Safety is raised only by events** (triggers with `satisfy` and a negative amount, as M6-19 plans for terror), and merges with `drive.terror`. Costs a change to the glossary: a need is a drive that can kill, rising with time or with what happens to the body.
3. **Safety is a desire** (it never kills). Cheapest; a body that feels unsafe flees through goal choice, never takes damage from the drive.

Recommendation: option 2, merged with M6-19's `drive.terror`, since that task already plans the triggers; option 1 can come later if shelter becomes a place in the world.

**Decided by the owner:** Option 2, event-driven and merged with terror. Safety rises only through triggers (being hit, a neighbour dying), not with time, and it is the same drive as M6-19's `drive.terror`. A need is a drive that can kill, rising with time or with what happens to the body. In `content/data/drives.cue`, `drive.safety` keeps its `rise: "0"` and its `TODO(M6-03.F1)` comment is removed; M6-19 attaches the triggers and thresholds to it rather than adding a separate terror drive.
