# M2-11.F1 · Stand and Give free a channel that still has a swing winding up

## Goal

A channel never has two actions in flight at once: either `ready` leaves a channel with a pending action alone, or the design says what Stand and Give do to a swing winding up on a channel they free, and the core does it.

## Context

- `content/data/forms.cue`: Stand ends with `{do: "ready", params: {channels: "self:all", count: "99"}}`, and Give with `{do: "ready", params: {channels: "target:all", count: "1"}}`.
- M2-11's random-fight property found it: Pin (arm 4), then Sever (arm 5), then Stand frees both arms at once, then Pin on arm 5 starts while the Sever is still winding up. Two pending actions then hold one channel (`comp.Landing.Channels`). Seed 0, picks `7, 31, 2, 12` of `TestRandomFightsKeepTheInvariants` reproduce it (the trail is `pin/8/10/4`, `sever/type.name/8/12`, `stand`, `pin/8/12/5`).
- The spec's property "no channel starts an action while it's busy" fails on it. The property test skips Stand and Give options until this is fixed (`readiesBusyChannels` in `internal/core/combat/property_test.go`, marked `TODO(M2-11.F1)`).

## Deliverables

- Decide (a question for the owner if the design is silent): does Stand free a limb mid-swing and let the swing still land, cancel it, or leave limbs with a swing in flight busy? Give the same for the one channel Give readies.
- Change the `ready` effect or the two forms to match, and add a test that Stand and Give leave no channel in two actions.
- Remove `readiesBusyChannels` from the property test.

## Spec

Most reversible meanwhile: `ready` skips a channel that has a pending action, so the swing keeps its limb until it lands.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `TestRandomFightsKeepTheInvariants` passes with Stand and Give among the options.
- [x] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Balancing Stand and Give (M7).

## Notes

Filed by M2-11.

Triaged 2026-11-23: todo in M2, because the owner chose to fix it in the current milestone.

Done: `ready` (`applyReady` in `internal/core/effects/timing.go`) skips a channel with a pending action, so the swing keeps its limb until it lands; the skipped channel does not count toward `count`. This is the spec's most reversible option, taken without asking the owner: if he would rather Stand cancel the swing or let it land with the limb free, that is a new decision. `readiesBusyChannels` is gone from the property test, and `TestReadyLeavesAChannelWithASwingWindingUp` covers the effect. No follow-ups filed.
