# M2-07.F1 · What does a party wipe mean for the story?

## Goal

Decide what happens when every member of the party is dead or downed, so that the placeholder in M2-07 can be replaced.

## Context

M2-07 publishes `PartyLost` when no member is left standing, and `game.Apply` then rejects every command with `text.game.lost`. That is the most reversible option, not a design: the game is simply over. The design says protected people are downed rather than killed ("Three tiers of people"), but not what a fully downed party leads to. The code is in `internal/core/combat/death.go` (`checkPartyLost`, `PartyLost`) and `internal/core/game/game.go` (`Apply`).

## Options

1. Game over (today). Cheap; a lost party is a hard stop and the player reloads. Fits a lethal game, but wastes a run on a downing that is supposed to be survivable.
2. Revive at a place. Downed members get up after a time or at a mark (a shrine, the last rest). Costs: a revive effect, a place to wake, and a rule for what the enemies did meanwhile.
3. The story takes over. Waking up captured, carried off by the deep, or in the next day phase with a cost (lost items, a graft). Costs: authored scenes or generated consequences per area.

## Recommendation

Option 1 until the story exists, then option 2 for downed members only (they are protected, so they are meant to come back) and keep game over for the case where nothing can revive them. The owner should decide before M3.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The owner picks an option and the choice is written into `docs/game-design.md`.
- [x] A task is filed for the chosen behavior.

## Out of scope

- Building any of the options.
