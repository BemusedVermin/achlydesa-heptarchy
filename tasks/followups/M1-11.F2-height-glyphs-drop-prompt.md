# M1-11.F2 · Plan view: ^ and v for height, red for out of reach, and ask before a drop

## Goal

The player can read height on the map at a glance: cells above the party draw `^`, cells below draw `v`, the color gets stronger the bigger the difference, rises get bolder the taller they are, and cells the party can't get to are red. Stepping off a ledge asks first, because a fall of two cells or more hurts.

## Context

- `docs/game-design.md`, **Reading height**: today's scheme, which tints cells cooler and darker below the party and warmer and lighter above, plus the heights overlay of digits. The owner finds it hard to read.
- `docs/go-technical-design.md`: the **Plan view** row of the pivots table, and the frontend section on the hex map ("Depth shows only as shading relative to the party").
- `internal/core/explore/project.go`: `buildMap` already asks `hexmap` for the cells the party can reach within `party.plan_range` (M1-10.F1.F1), and draws a hex's reachable cell first.
- `internal/frontend/tui/render.go`: `shade`, `paint` and the heights overlay.
- Step options come from content actions through `actions` (`buildOptions` in `internal/core/actions/options.go`), checked by the `step` effect primitive in `internal/core/explore/install.go`. Every option a key or the action menu picks goes through `App.Choose` in `internal/frontend/tui/app.go`.
- `content/data/templates.cue`, `_fallInjury`: a fall of two cells or more damages the legs.
- `party.cue`: `max_step_up: 1`, `max_step_down: 1`, `max_drop: 8`, `plan_range: 12`.

## Spec

Decided by the owner, 2026-11-27:

1. **Glyphs.** Every visible open cell higher than the party draws `^`, and every one lower draws `v`. Level cells keep their terrain's floor glyph, and walls keep their wall glyph. Entities still draw over terrain. The heights overlay of digits stays as it is.
2. **Color intensity.** The color gets more intense as the height difference grows, in both directions: one step is a light tint, `maxShade` steps the full hue. Above and below keep distinct hues (warm above, cool below). This replaces today's darkening of cells below; level cells are not tinted. Remembered cells are still dimmed.
3. **Boldness.** Rises go dim, normal or bold by how far above the party they are, with the cut-offs as content numbers. Cells below use color intensity only.
4. **Red.** A visible open cell the party can't reach is drawn red: within `party.plan_range` hexes of the focus, the drawn cell is not in the set `hexmap` reported as reachable. Cells past the range and remembered cells are never red. A cell reachable only by a detour longer than the range reads as red; that's accepted.
5. **Asking before a drop.** Choosing a step the core calls a drop (`MoveDrop`, two or more cells down with current content) asks "Jump down N cells?" first, with No as the default. The question applies to every way of choosing the option: the step keys, the w/s zigzag and the action menu. Steps that aren't drops never ask. The question is only in the view: commands, agents and the harness are unchanged.

Suggestions, not decisions (the most reversible choices; say in Notes if you change one):

- The glyphs and cut-offs are content (rule 4), set by the core, so the frontend draws `CellView.Glyph` as it does now. In `party.cue`, beside `plan_range`, with placeholder numbers:

  ```cue
  plan_glyphs: {up: "^", down: "v"}
  // A rise of at least normal cells draws at normal weight, at least bold cells bold, and less dim.
  plan_rise: {normal: 2, bold: 4}
  ```

- New fields on `api.CellView`:

  ```go
  // Weight is how strongly to draw the cell: -1 dim, 0 normal, 1 bold.
  Weight int `json:"weight,omitempty"`
  // Unreachable is true for a visible open cell within the plan range that the party can't walk to.
  Unreachable bool `json:"unreachable,omitempty"`
  ```

- The question goes on `api.Option` so any later option can ask one too, not only steps:

  ```go
  // Confirm is the rendered question to ask before choosing the option; empty when it needs none.
  Confirm string `json:"confirm,omitempty"`
  ```

  `effects.Primitive` gets an optional `Confirm func(ctx *Context, p Params) api.Text`, beside `Check`, that `buildOptions` renders into `Option.Confirm`. The `step` primitive returns `text.explore.confirm_drop` with a `cells` argument for a drop, and an empty text otherwise.
- The popup is a `Screen` pushed on the app's stack like `tickPopup`: y or Enter sends the option, and n or Esc closes it with nothing sent. Its key help is frontend chrome; the question comes from the view.
- In `render.go`, red is drawn unshaded with `lookupColor("red")`, still at the cell's weight. Weight maps to Lip Gloss's `Faint` and `Bold`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Walking `place.test.descent` up and down its slope, cells above the party draw `^`, cells below draw `v`, and level cells keep their floor glyph.
- [ ] Color intensity grows with the height difference above and below, and level cells are untinted.
- [ ] Rises are dim, normal or bold by the content cut-offs; cells below are never bold or dim.
- [ ] A visible open cell within the plan range that the party can't reach is red; cells past the range and remembered cells never are.
- [ ] Choosing a drop by step key, by zigzag or from the action menu opens "Jump down N cells?" from content text. No, n or Esc sends nothing; y or Enter takes the step. A level step, a climb and a one-cell step down never ask.
- [ ] The harness's output is unchanged (only the view gains fields).
- [ ] The heights overlay still draws digits.
- [ ] `docs/game-design.md` and `docs/go-technical-design.md` describe the new scheme.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- A setting to turn the drop question off.
- Asking before other risky options; the `Confirm` field leaves room for them.
- Reachability past `plan_range`, and caching it (M1-10.F1.F1.F1).
- The Ebitengine frontend.
