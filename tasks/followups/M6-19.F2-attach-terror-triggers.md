# M6-19.F2 · Attach the terror trigger and drive.safety to the templates that get minds

## Goal

Templates whose bodies run the mind controller list `drive.safety` in `drives` and `_terrorOnHit` in `triggers`, so being hit makes them flee.

## Context

- `content/data/templates.cue` defines `_terrorOnHit`, attached to nothing: no template has a mind yet, and adding a trigger to an existing template changes its saved trigger IDs and the goldens.
- M6-19 gives the goals, operators and pickers; no template carries a mind until M6-20, M6-21, M6-22 and M6-23.

## Deliverables

- `drives: "drive.safety": <weight>` and `_terrorOnHit` on each template made a mind by M6-21 to M6-23.

## Spec

Pick weights per template family. Not a design decision beyond numbers for tuning.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each mind template lists drive.safety and the trigger.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- A neighbour's death: M6-19.F1.
