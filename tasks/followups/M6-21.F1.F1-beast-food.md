# M6-21.F1.F1 · Decide how a beast meets its hunger

## Goal

The owner picks how a beast on the mind controller gets `drive.food` and meets it.

## Context

- The beast runs on the mind controller with no drive: it only fights and wanders. `drive.food` would starve it in about ten days unless a recipe can meet it.
- The eat recipes (`content/data/recipes/eat.cue`) need `action.take` and `action.consume`, which the beast's body may lack an action grant for.

## Deliverables

- The chosen option, as drives, operators and recipes in content.

## Spec

Options:

1. A larder raid: the beast goes to food affordances like a person. Cost: needs the take and consume actions on its body; beasts then raid towns.
2. A corpse: the beast eats corpses with an `operator.consume_corpse` like clay, met by `drive.food`. Cost: corpses compete with clay's drive.
3. No hunger: beasts stay drive-less, as now. Cost: they are the one family with no needs.

Recommendation: 2, since it reuses the corpse picker and needs no new grant.

**Decided by the owner:** Option 2, a corpse. A beast eats corpses with an `operator.consume_corpse`, met by `drive.food`, the same as clay. Content gives the beast `drive.food` and the operator; it needs no take or consume grant. Corpses now feed both clay and beasts; `docs/game-design.md` ("Monsters with needs") says so.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The owner has chosen, and the beast acts on it.

## Out of scope

- Soul-feeders and wish-born creatures.
