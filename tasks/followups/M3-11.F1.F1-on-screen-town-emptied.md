# M3-11.F1.F1 · Decide whether a town the party is in emits TownEmptied when its last resident leaves

## Goal

Settle whether a town can be "emptied" while the party is in it, so M3-12's replacement timers and the harness's "towns emptied" column (M3-20) count the same thing.

## Context

- `internal/core/agents/offscreen.go`, `applyPlan`: `TownEmptied` fires only from the off-screen tick, on the phase a town's resident count reaches 0 from above 0.
- `internal/core/agents/leave.go`, `crossGate`: a resident body leaving the party's town through a gate now publishes `Migrated`, but nothing publishes `TownEmptied` when the last one goes. The party's own region is never ticked, so the town is not reported as emptied until... never: once the party leaves, its count is already 0, and the tick only fires on a drop from above 0.
- `docs/game-design.md`: "An abandoned town stops replacing them, so emptied towns stay empty." `docs/go-technical-design.md` lists "towns emptied" among the harness's columns.

## Deliverables

- The decision recorded in `docs/game-design.md` (Towns) and, if yes, a task to emit it.

## Spec

Options:

1. **Yes, emit on screen too.** When the last body of the town's resident family leaves the town's map, and the region counts none of that family, publish `TownEmptied` once. Cost: a check in `crossGate` (and on death, if dying counts); the harness and M3-12 see one definition. Recommended: "emptied towns stay empty" should not depend on where the party stood.
2. **No, only off screen.** A town the party watches empty is not marked emptied. Cost: none now; M3-12 and the harness miss towns that empty while the party is there, and such a town keeps replacing its NPCs.
3. **Only on screen by exodus, not by death.** As option 1, but a town whose residents die in front of the party is not "emptied". Cost: two notions of empty for designers to keep straight.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The owner has picked an option and it is in `docs/game-design.md`.

## Out of scope

- Generic NPC replacement itself (M3-12).

## Notes

Filed while making gate crossings publish `Migrated`; M3-11.F1 emits no `TownEmptied` meanwhile, the most reversible choice.
