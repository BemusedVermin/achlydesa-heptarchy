# M3-20.F1 · Decide what counts as degenerate camping

## Goal

Settle which concentrations of a family in one town are degenerate, so the 100-seed check in M3-20 can pass or fail on a design the owner chose.

## Context

- M3-20 defines camping as a family with more than `camping_share` of its living members in one town for more than `camping_days` days in a row.
- On the sample world, resting 30 days, every seed camps by that definition. Seed 1 to 5, with share 0.9 and days 10: `family.people` sits in the lake town from day 8 or 9 on, because the shallow town's residents die out by day 9; `family.soul_feeder` sits in the lake town from day 2 to 5 on, as its drift sends it toward the living; on some seeds `family.people.guards` sits in the shallow town for days 2 to 12 because it starts there alone.
- The first two are what the design asks for: people leave a town short of water, and soul feeders go where the living are.

## Deliverables

- A decision, recorded in `docs/game-design.md`, then applied to the harness's check.

## Spec

Options:

1. Keep the definition and tune the sample world until no family camps. Costs content changes in M7 and may remove the water-shortage drama.
2. Count only a family whose members could be elsewhere: skip a family whose other members all died, and any family that starts in one town. Costs a less literal check; a town that lives while another dies is not flagged.
3. Exempt the families whose content says they follow something (soul feeders, people following water), and flag the rest.

Recommendation: option 2, with the exemption for a family that starts in one town.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The owner has chosen an option and the design doc says which.

## Out of scope

- Tuning the numbers (M7).
