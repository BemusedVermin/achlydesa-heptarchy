# M1-15.F1 · Rebuild the turn timeline after loading a save

## Goal

A world restored from a save resumes its turns where they left off. The turn heap and the current actor live in an Ark resource, not in the saved components, so a load has to rebuild them from `comp.Turn`.

## Context

- M1-15 keeps the timeline as a min-heap in the `turns` resource, filled by the spawner and `EndTurn`.
- M1-12 (save) restores entities and components directly, which bypasses the spawner.

## Deliverables

- `internal/core/turns`: an exported `Rebuild(w *sim.World)` that clears the heap and current actor, then pushes every entity with a closed `comp.Turn` and sets the current actor from the one with `Open` set.
- A test that saves mid-turn, loads into a fresh world, and gets the same turn order.

## Spec

`Rebuild` is the only addition; M1-12 calls it after restoring components.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two worlds, one restored from a snapshot, produce the same turn order over 1,000 turns.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- The save format itself (M1-12).

## Notes

Obsolete: M1-12 restores the turn queue, the current body and the pending flag exactly through `timeline.Export` and `timeline.Import` (`internal/core/timeline/save.go`), so there is nothing left to rebuild. Closed by the owner's request while reviewing PR #20.
