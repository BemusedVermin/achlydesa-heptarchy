# M6-22.F2 · Tune how a settlement lays pieces when a landmark is lacking

## Goal

Laying a settlement succeeds without oversized radii and street counts, and does not depend on the seed.

## Context

- `internal/core/worldgen/settlement.go`: while a landmark is lacking, only pieces giving it are candidates, so the street never grew and a town stopped at about six pieces. M6-22 lets the street piece fill in when no lacking piece fits, and counts the affordances of a piece's spawned templates.
- The three towns use radius 30 and up to 20 streets as first values; smaller radii failed on some seeds with "no room left".

## Deliverables

- Lay streets in step with other pieces, or search more doors, so a smaller radius works on every seed; tune the three towns.

## Spec

None beyond the goal.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every town lays out on at least 100 seeds at a smaller radius.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- New piece kinds: M6-23 and later.
