# M3-16.F1 · Raise terror in the bodies that witness an event, not those in the striker's sight

## Goal

A recruit's terror rises when it sees an ally maimed or killed, whoever or whatever did it.

## Context

- `content/data/templates.cue`: `_severedTerror` and `_deathTerror` use `satisfy` with `bodies: "everyone"`.
- `internal/core/effects/selectors.go`, `bodiesInSight`: every body selector except `self` and `target` looks from `ctx.Source`.
- `PartSevered` with no `Source` (worn away) resolves no bodies, so no recruit is shaken by it. `BodyDied` was given its own body as source in `Subjects()` to make the death trigger work.

## Deliverables

- A body selector (say `witnesses`) that looks from the event's target, or an `effects` change that makes the selectors fall back to the target when there is no source.
- `_severedTerror` and `_deathTerror` use it; `BodyDied.Subjects()` drops the source.

## Spec

Suggestion: add `BodiesWitnesses` to `BodySet`, resolved like `everyone` but from `ctx.Target`. Keep the existing selectors as they are.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A limb worn away with no source still raises the terror of recruits in sight of the body.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Terror from other causes.
