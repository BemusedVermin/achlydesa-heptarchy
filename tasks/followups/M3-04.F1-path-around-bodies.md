# M3-04.F1 · Path around standing bodies in the behavior primitives

## Goal

`move_toward`, `consume`, `carry` and `guard` do not stall behind a body that stands on their route.

## Context

- `approach` in `internal/core/agents/primitives.go` calls `Map.Path` with a nil cost, so the route ignores bodies. The step into an occupied cell is then not startable, and the body waits at the same cell for good.
- Seen in M3-04's hauler test: a well body stood on the straight route to the cistern, and the hauler stopped next to it. The test places the well off the route instead. The `hunt` controller's `stepToward` has the same gap.

## Deliverables

- A cost function for `Map.Path` that prices cells holding a standing body, used by `approach`.
- A test where a body stands on the straight route and the walker still arrives.

## Spec

Price an occupied cell high rather than forbidding it, so a body in a corridor still finds the way once the blocker moves. Suggestion: a cost of a few steps.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A hauler whose straight route passes through a well body still delivers.
- [x] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- The `hunt` controller's own stepping, which lives in `combat`.

## Notes

- `approach` now prices a cell held by another body (position and actor) at 5 instead of 1, except the goal cell. Unit of the price is `bodyDetour` in `primitives.go`.
- Test: `TestAHaulerGoesAroundAWallOfStandingBodies` puts a row of idle bodies across the straight route; it fails without the cost.
