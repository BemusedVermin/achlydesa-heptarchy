# M2-06.F1 · Should Strike and Sever differ on a weakness?

## Goal

Decide whether Strike (`weak ? 99 : 3`) and Sever (`damage 1`, then `sever when weak`) should behave differently on a weakness, or share one rule.

## Context

- M2-06's forms are placeholders. On a weakness Strike takes a part off by wearing it away (cause `worn`), while Sever cuts it (cause `cut`) but barely hurts it otherwise.
- Game design: "Combat → Forms", "Combat → Lethality".

## Deliverables

- A decision recorded in the design doc, and the form numbers changed to match (M7 tuning).

## Spec

Options: keep the difference (Strike wounds, Sever cuts clean); make both cut on a weakness; or make Sever the only form that cuts. Meanwhile the most reversible choice stands: keep as is.
