# M2-06.F1 · Should Strike and Sever differ on a weakness the way these placeholders do?

## Goal

The owner settles how a weakness reads for each form, so M7 tunes from a decision rather than a placeholder.

## Context

- M2-06's placeholders: Strike on a weakness takes the part off in one blow (`rule.hit` is 99); Sever wears a part by 1 and cuts it off outright on a weakness (`sever(when: rule.weak)`). Both end with a part coming off, so the difference is only that Sever also wears a part that isn't weak and is slower to land (2 against 3) but slower to recover (5 against 4).
- Game design, "Combat → Moves = form × type".

## Deliverables

- A decision recorded in `docs/game-design.md`, and then a content change in `content/data/forms.cue` and `content/data/rules.cue` if the answer is not "keep".

## Spec

Open question. Options: keep both as they are; make Strike never cut on a weakness but hit hard (e.g. `rule.weak ? 6 : 3`), leaving cutting to Sever; or make Sever the only form that takes a part off.
