# M5-10.F2.F1 · Choose another way to open a sealed pocket

## Goal

An export with the party in a closed chamber still leaves a route to a town.

## Context

- The owner decided to widen the pocket (M5-10.F2, option 3). In `pocketExits` (`internal/core/explore/export.go`) a reached standable cell is an exit when any neighbor lies outside the pocket, whatever that neighbor is. So the exit list is empty only when the region the party can walk to is closed on every side by solid cells inside the pocket.
- Widening adds old ground the party cannot walk to. The region stays closed at every radius, so no exit appears and the promise in `docs/game-design.md` ("An export with the party below") is still broken. Widening is a no-op for this case.

## Deliverables

- A decision, then the change.

## Spec

Options:

1. Open a tunnel from the party's region through solid cells to the nearest old ground or pocket edge: keeps the promise, edits the pocket's own cells.
2. Widen, and also tunnel through the widened ground: the pocket stays as it was, and only the added ground is cut. Costs keeping more of the old map.
3. Leave it: the party digs or waits for the next export.

Recommendation: 2, so the pocket's own cells stay as they were. The design text would then say "a tunnel is cut through the surrounding old ground".

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A test with a closed chamber has a route out after an export.
- [x] `docs/game-design.md` says how.
- [x] `go tool task check` passes.
