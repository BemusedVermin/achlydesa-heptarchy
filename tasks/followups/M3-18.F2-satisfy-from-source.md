# M3-18.F2 · Let a trigger satisfy a need of the acting body

## Goal

A soul-feeder's need.soul falls when it wounds the living, so its hunger can be met and can matter to the chooser.

## Context

- `satisfy` in `internal/core/agents/needs.go` lowers the level of `ctx.Target` only. A trigger on `combat.part_damaged` with role `source` has the damaged body as its target, so it cannot feed the attacker.
- `need.soul` in `content/data/needs.cue` only rises; `behavior.feeder.hunt` already scales with it.

## Deliverables

- `internal/core/agents/needs.go`: a `bodies` param (default the target, as now) taking `self` or `target`, as `damage` does for parts.
- `content/data/templates.cue`: a trigger on the soul-feeder that satisfies `need.soul` on a hit.
- Tests.

## Spec

A suggestion: `satisfy` takes an optional `bodies: "self"`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A soul-feeder that wounds someone has a lower soul level afterward.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Soul-feeders growing stronger near the dying (design sketch, later).
