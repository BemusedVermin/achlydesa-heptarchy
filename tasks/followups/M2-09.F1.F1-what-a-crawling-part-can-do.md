# M2-09.F1.F1 · What can a crawling part do?

## Goal

The design says what a severed part that crawls off can do in a fight, so its `crawls_as` template can be finished.

## Context

- `docs/game-design.md`, combat: "A part severed from one sometimes crawls off and fights as its own combatant, so cutting can multiply enemies." It doesn't say with what.
- `docs/game-design.md`, canon table: "In the deep, anyone who stands freezes everyone who crawls." Stand freezes bodies whose template carries the `crawls` tag (`content/data/forms.cue`).
- M2-09.F1 added `crawls_as` on a part template and `template.crawling.remnant_arm` in `content/data/templates.cue`. Meanwhile it knows only `action.strike`, loaded with `type.cut`, and has no `crawls` tag.
- Only `part.remnant_arm` has a `crawls_as`. `part.remnant_core` has none, so if the remnant's crawl trigger fires for a severed core, the body is named through `text.template.part.remnant_core`, which doesn't exist. Whether a vital core should crawl at all belongs with these answers (a trigger condition such as `integrity > 0`, or a `crawls_as` for the core).

## Deliverables

- The answers recorded in `docs/game-design.md`, and `template.crawling.remnant_arm` (and any other crawling template) changed to match.

## Spec

Three questions, with options:

1. **Which forms?**
   - (a) Strike only (today). Cheap and readable: a crawling arm is a weak extra enemy.
   - (b) Strike and Sever: a crawling arm can take parts off too, which makes cutting a remnant much riskier.
   - (c) The forms the part's grants allow, like any body (Strike, Sever, Pin with one action grant). Most consistent, most dangerous.
   - Recommendation: (a) until M7's lethality tuning shows the encounter is too easy.
2. **Which type does it swing with?** Cut (today), or a type of its own that matches old matter (a new type record). Recommendation: Cut for now; a new type is a content addition later.
3. **Does a crawling part crawl, for Stand?** The canon says standing freezes everyone who crawls, and a severed arm crawls. Adding the `crawls` tag to the crawling template makes Stand freeze it. Recommendation: yes, it reads as the canon intends, but it is the owner's call because it makes Stand a direct counter to remnants.

**Decided by the owner (question 3 only):** No, a crawling part does not crawl for Stand. The crawling template does not get the `crawls` tag, so Stand does not freeze severed remnants. This is what `template.crawling.remnant_arm` does today, so nothing changes in content. Questions 1 (which forms) and 2 (which type) are still open, so the task stays `proposed`.

Movement is separate: a lone arm has no legs grant, so it cannot step; whether a crawling part can move at all is a further question this one leaves to M7.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The owner has answered all three, and the answers are in `docs/game-design.md`.
- [ ] `template.crawling.remnant_arm` matches them.

## Out of scope
