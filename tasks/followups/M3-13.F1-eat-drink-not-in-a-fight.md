# M3-13.F1 · Let an action's requires formula see whether the party is on alert

## Goal

`action.eat` and `action.drink` are refused while a hostile is in sight, as M3-13's spec asks, with the rule written in content.

## Context

- M3-13 says eating and drinking are available "while not in a fight's reach". `content.ActionEnv` and `BodyEnv` carry no alert fact, so the actions only require the item (`content/data/actions.cue`).
- `comp.Party.Alert` holds the fact (`internal/core/explore`).

## Deliverables

- A field on `BodyEnv` or `ActionEnv` (for example `source.alert`) filled from the body's party.
- `requires` on `action.eat` and `action.drink` reads it.

## Spec

Suggestion: `BodyEnv.Alert bool`, false for a body outside a party. Foundation code (`effects/env.go`) reads `comp.Party` through `anatomy.PartyOf`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] With a hostile in sight, the eat and drink options are disabled with a reason.
- [x] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Whether eating mid-fight should ever be allowed (a design question).

## Notes

Triaged 2026-11-24: todo in M3, because it makes the M3-13 rule that the party cannot eat or drink in sight of a hostile real.

- **Alert fact:** `BodyEnv.Alert` (`source.alert`, `target.alert`), filled in `effects.BodyEnvOf` from the body's party's `comp.Party.Alert`; false outside a party.
- **Deviation: a new action field.** An action whose `requires` is false is left out of the menu, not disabled, so putting `!source.alert` in `requires` would hide eat and drink rather than show them disabled with a reason. Actions gain `allowed` (Expr bool over `ActionEnv`) and `refusal` (a text ID, default `text.actions.requires`): a false `allowed` keeps the option, disabled, and a `Do` of it is rejected with the refusal. `action.eat` and `action.drink` keep the item check in `requires` (no ration: no option) and add `allowed: "!source.alert"` with `refusal: "text.actions.on_alert"` ("Not with a hostile in sight."). This is the most reversible shape: an action without `allowed` behaves as before. The owner may prefer a different field name.
- **Files outside the list:** `internal/content/{types.go,actions.go}` and generated schemas, `internal/core/actions/actions.go` (evaluates `allowed`), `content/data/text/actions.cue`, `docs/content-guide.md` (documents `requires` versus `allowed` and `source.alert`), `internal/frontend/tui/testdata/menu_actions.golden` (that menu is drawn mid-fight, so eat and drink now show disabled).
- **Tests:** `TestEatingAndDrinkingAreRefusedWithAHostileInSight` (agents), `TestFailingAllowedDisablesTheOptionWithTheActionsRefusal` (actions).
- **Follow-ups:** none.
