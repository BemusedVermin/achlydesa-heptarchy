# M3-16.F2 · Decide how deserters stand toward the party

## Goal

`faction.deserters` has a settled relation to the party and to the people.

## Context

- `docs/game-design.md` → "Recruits" says a deserter lives on as an individual; "Grudges and memory" (M6) covers how others remember it.
- `content/data/factions.cue`: `faction.deserters` is hostile to no one, and no one is hostile to it.

## Deliverables

- The chosen relation in `factions.cue`, or a grudge rule in M6.

## Spec

Options:

1. Hostile to no one (now). Cheapest; a deserter wanders the town and never fights, so nothing punishes leaving unpaid.
2. Hostile to the party. A deserter that meets the party fights it, so desertion has a cost. Risks a fight in town the player cannot avoid.
3. Neutral until M6's grudges decide, per individual.

Recommendation: option 3, so the answer comes from memory and not from one faction flag. Option 1 stays until then.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The owner has chosen, and the choice is in the docs.

## Out of scope

- Deserters taking seats (M4).

## Notes

The owner chose option 3: deserters stay neutral until M6 grudges decide, per individual. `faction.deserters` is unchanged; `docs/game-design.md` "Recruits" records it.
