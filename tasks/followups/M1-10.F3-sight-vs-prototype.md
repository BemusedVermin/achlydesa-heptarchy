# M1-10.F3 · Sight differs from the prototype at pillar edges

## Goal

Decide whether `hexmap.Visible` should match the prototype's shadowcasting field of view, and make the two agree or record why not.

## Context

- M1-10 asks that the hall's visible cells match the prototype's field of view. `hexmap.Visible` is line based (M1-14) and the prototype uses the hex library's shadowcasting.
- With a party of one in the test hall they agree on all but 6 of about 120 hexes: four the prototype sees and `Visible` does not (such as relative hex (2, 2), behind the pillar at (1, 2)), and two the other way. M1-10's test allows up to 10% to differ.

## Deliverables

- Either `Visible` changed to shadowcast with depth, or a note in the tech design that line sight is the rule, and the test tightened to what is decided.

## Spec

Shadowcasting is more permissive around pillars; line sight is symmetric. Pick by how the pillars should feel.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The hall test compares the two fields of view with an exact or documented tolerance.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Sight across map links (M1-14.F1).
