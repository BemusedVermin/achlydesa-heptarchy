# M1-10.F1 · Plan view: a slope's ceiling beats its floor

## Goal

Decide how the plan view should draw a downhill slope, so it reads as one floor that gets gradually lower, as the task intends, instead of a run of walls.

## Context

- M1-10's projection rules (firm): candidates are standable cells and solid cells beside open cells; the visible candidate nearest the focus member in depth wins; ties go to the shallower.
- A walkable downhill staircase needs air in the cell beside the lip at the walker's depth (`hexmap.Step` refuses to step diagonally down into a solid cell). So the ceiling cell above each next slope step is a solid cell with an open neighbour: a wall candidate at `Below` 0, nearer than the floor one or more depths down.
- Walking `map.test.descent`'s slope, with `Below` per hex from the hall opening, the row reads like this (`.` floor, `#` wall):
  `.+0 .+0 .+1 #+0 #+1 #+2 #+3`, then `.-1 #+0 .+0 #-1 #+0 #+1 #+2` on the next step. The leader's own hex and the hexes behind it are floor, and the hexes ahead are walls at the leader's depth. No hex is missing, but the slope does not read as a floor getting lower.
- The acceptance criterion "continuous floor with `Below` changing smoothly" is met only as "no gap". M1-10 implements the rules as written and tests for no gap.

## Deliverables

- A decision, then the change to `explore/project.go` and its tests.

## Spec

Options:

1. **Standable beats wall** when both are visible in the same hex. Slopes read as floor. Cost: a wall of a pit or a cliff face next to a floor is hidden whenever the floor is visible; a low cell behind a ledge can be drawn over the wall in front of it.
2. **Walls count only when no standable cell is visible within N depths** of the focus. Keeps most walls, and slopes read as floor if N is at least the slope's rise per hex. Cost: one more tuning number.
3. **Keep the rules.** Cost: slopes read as walls ahead of the party.

Recommendation: option 2 with N from content (`party.sight_depth` is too large; start at 2).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Walking the descent's slope draws floor glyphs on every slope hex, with `Below` rising by one per hex.
- [ ] The hall still draws its walls and pillars.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- The terminal's shading of `Below` (M1-11).

## Notes

Decision (owner): option 2, with N from content, starting at 2. Closes the question; implement it.
