# M6-21.F1 · Run the creature families on the mind controller

## Goal

Moving clay, remnants and beasts run on `actor.controller: "mind"`, so their drives and recipes drive them in the real game, not only in tests.

## Context

- M6-21 gave the three templates a `mind` (planner `htn`) and the clay and remnant their drives, but left `actor.controller: "hunt"` (`_#Minded` in `content/data/templates.cue`). Switching broke the combat, den and tui goldens: a mind fights only through `goal.fight`, which waits on `think_ticks` and sight, so `TestEachCreatureOfTheDenSpawnsAndFightsBack` failed for the beast and remnant.
- Beasts carry no drive yet. `drive.food` would starve a hunt-controlled beast in about ten days, and its eat recipes need `action.take` and `action.consume`, which need an action grant their body may lack.
- M6-19.F2 attaches `drive.safety` and `_terrorOnHit` to mind templates; do both together.

## Deliverables

- `_#Minded` sets `actor: controller: "mind"`; goldens and fight tests updated, with the reason for each change in Notes.
- Beasts get `drive.food` and a way to meet it (a larder raid or a corpse), decided first.

## Spec

Firm: the lethality targets in the harness still hold, or Notes says which number moved.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `go tool task check` passes with the families on the mind controller.

## Out of scope

- Soul-feeders, wish-born creatures and the flock (M7, M8).
