# M1-16.F1 · Followers and swaps across map links

## Goal

A party in single file crosses a link to another map without the followers getting stuck, and a leader can swap with a follower across one.

## Context

- M1-16's `follow` controller steps into the cell the member ahead left, or paths toward it with `hexmap.Path`. `Trail.Last` is a bare cell with no map, and `Path` stays on one map (M1-14.F1).
- A follower whose leader has gone through a link finds the trail cell unreachable and no route, so it waits where it is.
- The step primitive refuses a swap when the step leaves the map, so the leader cannot step back into a follower's cell across a link.

## Deliverables

- `internal/core/comp/comp.go`: `Trail` gains the map of the cell.
- `internal/core/explore/follow.go`, `install.go`: step into a trail cell through a link; swap across one.

## Spec

Once M1-14.F1 gives `Path` links, the follower paths through them. `Trail{Map, Last}` lets the follower step through the link the leader used. Whether a swap across a link is allowed is a design question: keep refusing until a fold ships.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A party of two crosses a link and the follower arrives in a test.
- [ ] `go tool task check` passes.

## Out of scope

- Sight across links (M1-14.F1).
