# M6 · Minds

**Status:** outline. Design it with the owner, then break it into tasks, once M5 is done.

**Goal:** creatures and people decide what to do for reasons of their own, so what happens in the world emerges from them (AGENTS.md rule 10). Nothing here is decided yet; the notes below are where the conversation stands.

**Design sources:** `docs/game-design.md` → "The living deep" (Outcomes emerge, Needs change, What happened is found), "Monsters with needs", "People with needs", "Party" → "Recruits".

## Ideas so far (not decisions)

- Needs are what it takes to stay alive. Wants and desires reflect personality.
- Goal-oriented action planning (GOAP) looks like a good fit: pick a goal, then plan the steps to it.
- Agents know where things are from what they've seen and heard. Knowing nothing, they pick a path at random.
- There is no leave or migrate: going elsewhere is just going for what they need.
- A recruit quitting is an act, like hiring.
